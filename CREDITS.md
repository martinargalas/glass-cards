# Credits

Glass cards are built on other people's work. Each card that uses one of these shows it in its
editor, in the **Credits** tab.

## Wraps

Cards that draw another card inside and set it up.

| Project | Author | Used by | What |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [apexcharts-card](https://github.com/RomRider/apexcharts-card) | [RomRider](https://github.com/RomRider) | `glass-period-chart` | Draws the chart; the glass card sets its span for the period and its look. Install it from HACS. |

## Requires

Cards that need another card installed to work.

| Project | Author | Used by | What |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Bubble Card](https://github.com/Clooos/Bubble-Card) | [Clooos](https://github.com/Clooos) | `glass-dashboard`, `glass-popup-header`, `glass-dock` | The pop-ups are Bubble Card's; the glass cards place and open them. Install it from HACS. |
